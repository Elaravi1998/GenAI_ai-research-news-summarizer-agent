# 📰 AI Research & News Summarizer Agent

> 🤖 An AI-powered research and news summarization system built with **Python, NLP, Generative AI, and AI Agent workflows**.

The **AI Research & News Summarizer Agent** collects relevant research/news content, ranks sources, summarizes information, identifies common themes, compares multiple sources, and generates a grounded research brief.

The project is designed to evolve from a simple Python notebook into a production-ready **AI Research Agent** using LLMs, RAG, LangGraph, MCP, and external research tools.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Researchers, developers, analysts, and businesses often need to process a large amount of information from:

- 📰 News articles
- 📚 Research papers
- 🌐 Web pages
- 📊 Industry reports
- 📄 Documents
- 🔬 Technical publications

Reading everything manually is time-consuming.

This project aims to automate the research workflow:

```text
Research Question
       ↓
Information Retrieval
       ↓
Source Ranking
       ↓
Content Extraction
       ↓
AI Summarization
       ↓
Multi-Source Synthesis
       ↓
Fact / Claim Grounding
       ↓
Research Brief
